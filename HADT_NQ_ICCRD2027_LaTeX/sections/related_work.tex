\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Deformable Attention}

Conventional attention mechanisms aggregate features from predefined token positions, whereas deformable attention adaptively adjusts sampling positions through learnable offsets. Zhu et al.~\cite{zhu2021deformable} proposed Deformable DETR, which aggregates informative features from a limited number of sampling points around reference positions. Subsequently, deformable attention has been introduced into time series forecasting. DeformableTST~\cite{deformabletst2024} employs adaptive temporal attention to reduce the dependence on predefined patching strategies, while DeformTime~\cite{shu2025deformtime} incorporates temporal and variable deformable attention to capture multivariate dependencies. These methods provide effective approaches to adaptive feature extraction, while the relationship between historical sampling positions and individual future time steps deserves further investigation.

\subsection{Non-crossing Quantile Regression}

Existing approaches to the quantile crossing problem mainly include post-processing methods and structurally constrained quantile regression. The former rearranges predicted quantiles after forecasting, whereas the latter imposes monotonicity through model parameterization. Cannon~\cite{cannon2018noncrossing} introduced monotonic constraints into neural quantile regression. Park et al.~\cite{park2022learning} developed incremental quantile functions to ensure quantile monotonicity through positive increments. Chen et al.~\cite{chen2025noncrossing} proposed an improved non-crossing quantile regression strategy based on exponential stacking mapping for wind power probabilistic forecasting. For general time series with different value ranges, constructing ordered quantiles without predefined output bounds provides greater flexibility.

Based on these studies, this paper investigates horizon-con\-di\-tioned historical feature sampling and median-anchored quantile parameterization for long-term probabilistic time series forecasting.
